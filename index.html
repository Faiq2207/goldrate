Common questions

What is the 24K gold rate in Pakistan today?

Today’s official 24K gold rate in Pakistan is Rs 429,136 per tola and Rs 367,914 per 10 gram, as issued by the All Pakistan Sarafa Gems and Jewellers Association on 7 November 2026. 22K is Rs 393,375, 21K is Rs 375,494, 18K is Rs 321,852 per tola.

Who sets the gold rate in Pakistan?

The All Pakistan Sarafa Gems and Jewellers Association announces the official daily gold rate, which jewellers across the country use as their reference.

What’s the difference between the live rate and the Sarafa rate?

The Sarafa rate is announced by the association, usually once a day. The live rate is our estimate that moves with the international gold price and the dollar rate throughout the day. Jewellers price from the Sarafa rate, so use it for buying and selling.

How is the live rate calculated?

We take the live international gold price per ounce, add the usual $20 premium the local market applies, convert it to tola (1 ounce is 31.1035 grams, 1 tola is 11.6638 grams) and multiply by the current dollar rate.

Why are the Rawalpindi market rates different?

The Sarafa market table shows the rates one Rawalpindi wholesaler actually trades at. Local rawa gold is not fully 24K, ARY bars carry a brand premium, and the 21K jewellery rate is set by the Rawalpindi Sarafa Association with its own buy-back deductions.

How many grams are in one tola?

One tola equals 11.6638 grams. One tola has 12 masha, and one masha has 8 ratti.

What is the nisab for zakat on gold?

The nisab for gold is 7.5 tola (about 87.48 grams). If you own this much gold or more for a full lunar year, zakat is 2.5% of its value.
